Predictions are the 8-seed ensemble mean from the headline homology-clean ESM2-3B transfer model (this project's recommended CryptoBench result). Crypticity labels (cryptic / pre-formed) come from an independent external audit that strictly relabeled the test split only — validation-split chains and the remaining test chains are unlabeled by that audit, not necessarily open pockets. ROC-AUC and PR-AUC are per-chain (omitted when a chain has no negative or no positive residues to compare). Center error is the distance in Å between the predicted and true pocket center; a hit is ≤4Å.
